Media plan by audience

Which channels carry each audience

How money is assigned: every channel line's money is split across the five audiences in proportion to where its impressions land. A Meta buy aimed at Wealth & Business Leaders still lands most of its impressions on Strivers, so most of its money counts towards Strivers. People reached 3+ is the average across the before and during windows. Cost per person = money assigned to the audience ÷ people in it reached 3+ times.

Regional view

Media lands by province in line with where each audience lives. TV is bought nationally and isn't tilted; every other channel can be weighted by province. Tilting moves weight, not money, so other provinces get a little less. Personal market share 2024 to 2025 from the IQBusiness Market Share Tracker (FNB regional view).

The model holds every plan to the same test, then stops spending where the return halves

Sufficient

Enough of each audience sees the campaign three or more times in four weeks, one pay cycle. Each exposure counts by its channel weight.

Lowest budget

The least money that meets every switched-on audience's target, before and during the holidays.

Recommended

The last R1m step where the next R1m still buys at least half of what the average R1m buys. Past it, money mostly adds frequency for people already reached.

Rules

Every channel keeps at least a quarter of its money in the holidays. Channel ranges keep the mix buyable. Reach per channel rises to a ceiling set by how many in each audience use it.
